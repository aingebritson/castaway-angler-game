# The Cayes research dossier

Species pass: 17 November 2026 (spec section 12). The 14 species first added for this region were checked against live sources, and all 52 of their source references are now read and dated. The 50 occurrence records at the four Cayes spots have **not** been checked yet; see "Still to do". Island species that reappear here were checked in `research/castaway-island/`.

## Overview

Port Esperanza is a lightly fictional fishing town on the Belize coast (17.25°N 88.0°W, `America/Belize`), reached by raft from the island (DECISIONS 17). It has four fishing spots:

| Spot | Zones | Real habitat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Town Pier | pilings, harbour, bait school | Harbour pier and its shade; sardine schools for the cast net |
| Mangrove Creek | roots, creek | Red mangrove prop roots and a deep creek bend |
| Patch Reef | reef top, reef edge, deep | Inner-lagoon patch reef with a ledge into deeper water |
| The Cut | channel, blue edge, blue water | A tidal channel through the barrier reef and the drop beyond it |

### Conservation and law

All IUCN statuses match FishBase (Red List version 2025-2): Nassau grouper CR, black grouper NT, hogfish VU, queen triggerfish NT, nurse shark VU, West Indian manatee VU, and the rest LC or not evaluated.

- **Nassau grouper:** release-only in the game. Belize actually allows it from April to November within a 51 to 76 cm slot, and closes the fishery from December to March. The note now says so. Making it release-only goes further than Belize law, which is defensible for a Critically Endangered fish.
- **Manatee:** Belize holds the largest share of the Endangered Antillean subspecies (EDGE of Existence). This confirms the note.

### Sources fixed

- **Florida Museum links:** they used scientific-name slugs that do not exist. All eight now point to the real common-name pages, and all eight were read.
- **IGFA entries:** unsourced "IGFA world records" entries were replaced by the reports the records came from.
- **Manatee status:** read from Wikipedia, because the Red List page refuses automated reads.

### Simulator effect

I ran the simulator at the four spots with the deckhand, skipper and master rigs, in January and June. Catch shares moved by under 2 points, and coins per hour by under 4%. The Town Pier, where the first shop rod's price was tuned (DECISIONS 18), changed by under 1%. Gray snapper, Nassau grouper and crevalle jack now reach their larger maximums.

## Open questions

1. **Nassau grouper release-only vs Belize's slot.** The game is stricter than the law. If the Cayes market should follow Belize exactly, Nassau grouper would be sellable from April to November between 51 and 76 cm.
2. **White grunt misses the 30% check** against FishBase's 4.4 kg maximum weight. The Florida Museum repeats that figure, but every published length-weight set gives 1.9–2.5 kg at 53 cm, and state rod records are about 2.4 kg. This is the same situation as the island's yellowfin mojarra. I kept the Carolinas set.
3. **Cero maximum length.** FishBase and the Florida Museum both give 183 cm TL, which is implausible for a 7.8 kg fish; it looks like a king mackerel figure. The game uses 90 cm FL, chosen so the only fork-length set passes the weight check. This needs a better source.
4. **Nurse shark maximum.** FishBase gives 430 cm TL. The game keeps 308 cm, the largest size widely reported. This is a sighting, so it only affects flavour.
5. **King mackerel maximum (173 cm FL)** has no direct source. NOAA gives "up to 5.5 feet" (168 cm), and FishBase gives 184 cm TL.
